# FamilyLink Live — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 06/11/2026.

FamilyLink Live ("the app") lets people who have agreed to connect share their location and messages. This policy
explains what we collect, why, who sees it and how to delete it.

## What we collect
| Data | Why | Who can see it |
|---|---|---|
| Name, email address and profile picture from your Google account | Sign-in, and to show who you are to your contacts | Your accepted contacts |
| Profile photo you upload (optional) | Shown in Settings and to your contacts | Your accepted contacts |
| **Precise location** while you share it (manually, or with *Live location sharing* on) | The live map and arrival/departure alerts | Only contacts you have **accepted**, and only while *Share my location* is on. An SOS always includes your location. |
| Places you save (name, position, radius, optional arrival message) | Geofence alerts | You. Your contacts see a place's **name** only inside an arrival/departure alert. |
| Messages you send | Private chat | You and the contact in that thread |
| Device push token | Delivering notifications | Our servers |
| Language and settings | Showing the app and notifications in your language | You |

We do **not** sell data, show ads or use your data for advertising or analytics.

## How it is stored and protected
Data is sent over HTTPS. Your account data is held in our database; alert history in Google Cloud Firestore; profile
photos in Microsoft Azure Blob Storage; sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; push notifications by
Firebase Cloud Messaging; the API runs on Render. These providers process data on our behalf.
A copy of recent chats, contacts and places is cached on your phone so the app works offline, and is erased when you
sign out.

## Your choices
* **Stop sharing:** switch off *Share my location* or *Live location sharing* in Settings at any time.
* **Remove a contact:** removing or declining a contact ends all sharing with them immediately.
* **Delete your account:** *Settings → Delete my account* permanently deletes your profile, contacts, places,
  location history, messages, push tokens, alert history and profile photo, and your Firebase sign-in. You can
  also ask us to do it by emailing the address below.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
